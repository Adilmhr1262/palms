Fractions Basics

Learning objectives

  1. Identify the numerator and denominator in a fraction.
  2. Represent simple fractions with diagrams.
  3. Compare two fractions with the same denominator.

Reading

A fraction describes a part of a whole. The number above the line is the numerator, and it counts how many parts we have. The number below the line is the denominator, and it tells us how many equal parts the whole is divided into. In the fraction 3/4, the whole is split into four equal parts and we have three of them.

When two fractions share the same denominator, the one with the larger numerator is the larger fraction. So 3/8 is greater than 1/8, because three parts out of eight is more than one part out of eight.
